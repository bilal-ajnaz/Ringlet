Ringlet support

Answers to the common questions. Still stuck? Write to bilalibnajnaz@gmail.com.

Can I use Apple Music songs?

Apple Music streams are protected by Apple and can’t be used. Songs stored on your device, files from Files and the sound from your videos all work.

How long can a ring be?

Pick 10, 15, 20 or 30 seconds. Ringtones for calls are 30 seconds or less.

How do I set a ring for calls?

Slide to make it ring. When the share sheet opens, tap Use as Ringtone. Then pick it in Settings › Sounds & Haptics, or on a contact’s card for just one person. iOS asks you to do this last step yourself.

Will my alarm ring on silent?

Yes. Ringlet asks for alarm permission the first time, then wakes you with your sound, even on silent.

How many rings can I make for free?

You can keep five rings of your own on a free shelf. The sample rings Ringlet starts you with don’t count. Ringlet Pro makes it unlimited.

What does Ringlet Pro include?

  • Unlimited rings.
  • Your look: the Holo finish, your own photo, your own words and five typefaces on the label.
  • Fades and effects: fade in and out, speed, pitch, and a boost for quiet sounds.
  • Make a beat: tap a rhythm onto a record and turn it into a ring.
  • Sound layers: choose which parts of a song play: vocals, drums, bass and melody.
  • Sound packs: four seasonal packs of ready-made tones.
  • Gentle wake-up: alarms start soft and rise over the first seconds.
  • iCloud sync: your rings on iPhone, iPad and Mac, kept in step.

It’s one purchase with no subscription. You can try the looks and effects before you buy.

I bought Ringlet Pro on another device

Ringlet Pro belongs to your Apple Account. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings in Ringlet and tap Restore purchase. On Mac, choose Ringlet › Settings › Ringlet Pro, then Restore Purchase.

How do I sync my rings between devices?

Sync comes with Ringlet Pro. Turn on iCloud sync in Ringlet’s Settings on each iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. Sample rings and alarms stay on each device.

How do I get a ring from my Mac onto my iPhone?

Connect your iPhone, select it in the Finder sidebar, then drag the ring from Ringlet’s sidebar onto the iPhone’s General tab. On iPhone, pick it in Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone.

Does Ringlet work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Your rings show up as records on the watch, and you can play them on its speaker.
