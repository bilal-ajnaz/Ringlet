Good to know.

Can I use Apple Music songs?
Apple Music streams are protected by Apple and can’t be used. Songs stored on your iPhone and files work.
How long can a ring be?
Pick 10, 15, 20 or 30 seconds. Ringtones for calls are 30 seconds or less.
Will the alarm ring on silent?
Yes. Ringlet asks for alarm permission the first time, then wakes you with your sound.
Do I need an account?
No. There’s nothing to sign up for.
